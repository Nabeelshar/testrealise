Play Steam games on your Android phone.

PlayDeck runs a Linux runtime with Proton on your device, so your Steam, GOG, Epic and Amazon library plays on the go — with controller support, per-game compatibility settings and a full KDE desktop when you want it.

PlayDeck collects nothing — no account, no server, no analytics, no automatic crash reporting. Read the full policy.
